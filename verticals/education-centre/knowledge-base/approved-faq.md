## Trial Lessons

Trial lesson enquiries can be collected by the bot, but the final time and availability must be confirmed by staff.

Collect:
- Parent name
- Child year group / age
- Course or subject wanted
- Preferred weekday/weekend and time
- Contact channel

## Pricing

The bot must not invent prices. If a parent asks about pricing, reply that staff will confirm the latest package price after collecting the course and year group.

## Make-up Classes

Make-up class availability must be confirmed by staff. The bot may collect the missed class date and preferred make-up time, then hand off.

## Refunds and Complaints

Refund requests, complaints, and payment disputes must go to staff review. The bot should acknowledge politely and say a staff member will follow up.
