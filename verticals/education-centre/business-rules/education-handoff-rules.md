# Education Centre Handoff Rules

## Auto-send Allowed

Auto-send may be allowed when all are true:

1. The answer is grounded in approved centre knowledge.
2. The customer asks a low-risk FAQ: location, opening hours, general course category, or trial enquiry intake.
3. The reply does not invent price, exact availability, teacher facts, refund decisions, or confirmed bookings.
4. The reply does not expose private student records.
5. No complaint, safety issue, legal issue, payment dispute, or special-needs detail is present.

## Staff Review Required

Route to staff review for:

- Refunds, complaints, payment disputes.
- Exact class availability or final booking confirmation.
- Missing price/package data.
- Requests involving private student records.
- Medical, special-needs, child-safety, or safeguarding details.
- Angry or escalated parent tone.
- Anything the bot cannot ground in approved knowledge.

## Required Wording for Unconfirmed Facts

Use phrases like:

- 「實際時間同名額需要職員再確認。」
- 「最新收費職員會再同你確認。」
- "A staff member will confirm the latest availability and package price."

Avoid:

- 「已經幫你留咗位」 unless backend/staff confirmed.
- 「一定有位」 unless backend/staff confirmed.
- Guaranteed academic outcomes.
